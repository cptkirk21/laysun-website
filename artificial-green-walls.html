Why Specify an Artificial Green Wall for Commercial Spaces

The artificial green wall has become the signature biophilic feature of modern commercial interiors — and the reason is simple economics. A living green wall demands irrigation plumbing, grow lighting, drainage, and a maintenance contract that runs for the life of the installation. A commercial artificial green wall delivers the same lush, layered foliage impact with none of the ongoing cost. LaySun's panels never need watering, never drop leaves onto a reception desk, never brown out in a low-light corridor, and never trigger a maintenance call-out. They install onto any substrate — drywall, timber batten, brick, or a freestanding frame — and trim cleanly to fit columns, soffits and irregular openings.

Modular Panels That Cover Any Wall

Every LaySun green wall style is supplied as a modular 100 × 100 cm (1 m²) panel with an interlocking grid backing. Panels clip together edge-to-edge so the foliage runs continuously with no visible seams, and they cut with secateurs or a utility knife to follow any wall dimension. A typical feature wall behind a reception desk uses 6–10 panels; a full-height restaurant or hotel-lobby installation may use several dozen. Because pricing is per panel, you can scope a small accent backdrop or an entire facade from the same product — and reorder to match exactly months later.

Foliage Styles for Every Interior

The range spans ten distinct looks. Moss and fern panels suit calm, tactile, acoustically-soft settings — spas, meeting rooms and boutique retail. Garden foliage and the vibrant medley give a fresh, all-purpose green for cafés, gyms and creative offices. Tropical, galaxy, woodland and forest greens deliver dense, premium, deep-pile coverage for hotel lobbies, restaurant features and luxury retail. Wildflower and the medley blends add floral colour for event spaces, bars and bridal suites. Most styles are UV-stabilised for bright interiors and covered exterior use — courtyards, terraces and shopfront facades.

Fire Rating, UV Stability and Commercial Compliance

NFPA 701 (National Fire Protection Association 701) fire-rated options are available as a special order for select products. Testing is performed by an independent laboratory accepted by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) in the Textiles and Films category. Documentation is available upon request and is provided for the project or order. Project requirements vary by jurisdiction and application. For eligible special-order panels, the specified material construction, testing, and documentation are confirmed for the project or order. UV-stabilised foliage resists fading in direct and reflected sunlight. Custom panel runs, bespoke foliage mixes and framed freestanding dividers are all accommodated factory-direct, with a typical lead time of 15–30 days.
